Loyalty & Churn

Weekly classification into New, Loyal, and Churned users using a 4-week lookback window.

New · Loyal · Churned Users per Week

Loyal = active in every one of the 4 preceding weeks  ·  Churned = active in prior 4 weeks but not this week  ·  New = first-time active

Power & Habitual Users

12-week rolling usage segments (vivainsights methodology). Power User: habit & avg ≥ 15 actions/wk  ·  Habitual: IsHabit (active in ≥9/12 weeks)  ·  Novice: avg ≥ 1
